Our Basic Approach

Multi-Model AI Stack

We combine top paid AI-models and specialist models (reasoning, summarization, extraction) to get the best answer for each task. Using multiple providers reduces hallucination risk and increases coverage — so you get more accurate, nuanced insights faster.

Extensive Web Search & Smart Scraping

Our automated crawlers and targeted scraping pull relevant content at scale (news, blogs, forums, institutional sites). We normalize and tag sources, then filter noise so only high-value evidence reaches the analysis stage.

Clean Data = Trustworthy Insights

Every insight includes source attributions and licensing notes. We respect copyright, follow robots.txt and API terms, and provide clients with a clear audit trail for every claim or chart.

Human Expert Review & Quality Assurance

AI drafts are always reviewed by subject-matter experts who verify claims, add nuance, and flag uncertainties. Final deliverables combine LLM speed with human judgment — the best of both worlds.
